### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 研究潜在损失、违约及风险传播。信用解释与反事实补救仍属于信用风险任务；存在故意欺诈或攻击时才转向金融安全。市场、流动性和系统性风险应分别定义事件与损失。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Findings / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Credit Risk and Default Prediction / 信用风险与违约预测 (2); Market Risk and Tail Losses / 市场风险与尾部损失 (1)。已标注的方法：Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (1); Tabular Learning / 表格学习 (1); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (1); Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (1); Fairness & Recourse / 公平性与算法追索 (1)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 关注违约与尾部事件、概率校准、成本敏感性、压力测试及跨期稳定性。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.2053.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.1466.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 加强尾部事件、跨机构与跨期检验，把概率校准、拒贷成本和压力情景纳入统一决策评价。

**Problem and subdirections.** This task concerns potential losses, default and risk propagation. Credit explanations and recourse remain credit-risk tasks; deliberate fraud or attacks belong to financial safety. Market, liquidity and systemic risks require distinct events and loss definitions.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / application. Frequent subcategories: Credit Risk and Default Prediction / 信用风险与违约预测 (2); Market Risk and Tail Losses / 市场风险与尾部损失 (1). Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.2053.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.1466.md). **Research opportunities (editorial).** Strengthen tail-event, cross-institution and temporal evaluation, integrating calibration, lending costs and stress scenarios into decision assessment.
