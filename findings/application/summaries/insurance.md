### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 以保险合同、保费、索赔、准备金及寿命风险为对象。客户营销、保险销售对话与欺诈检测分别与金融服务或金融安全关联，不因使用保险数据就视为精算贡献。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Findings / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Claim Frequency and Loss Severity / 索赔频率与损失严重度 (2); Insurance Pricing and Premium Setting / 保险定价与保费厘定 (1)。已标注的方法：Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (2); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (2); Tabular Learning / 表格学习 (1); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (1); Optimization & Numerical Methods / 优化与数值方法 (1)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 关注索赔分布、校准、选择偏差、长期风险及业务约束。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.2053.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.2198.md) · [3](../indexes/finance_notes/2021.findings-acl.51.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 联合考察长尾赔付、风险选择和长期校准，检验模型在保单结构与人群变化下的适用性。

**Problem and subdirections.** This task concerns insurance contracts, premiums, claims, reserves and longevity risk. Marketing, sales dialogue and fraud detection connect to services or safety; using insurance data alone does not establish an actuarial contribution.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / application. Frequent subcategories: Claim Frequency and Loss Severity / 索赔频率与损失严重度 (2); Insurance Pricing and Premium Setting / 保险定价与保费厘定 (1). **Data and evaluation.** Inspect claim distributions, calibration, selection bias, long-term risk and business constraints. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.2053.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.2198.md) · [3](../indexes/finance_notes/2021.findings-acl.51.md). **Research opportunities (editorial).** Examine heavy-tailed claims, risk selection and long-term calibration, including changes in contract structure and insured populations.
