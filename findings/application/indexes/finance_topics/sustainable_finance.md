### English

**Problem and subdirections.** This task studies how sustainability information enters financing, investment and risk analysis. ESG disclosure, greenwashing, climate risk and responsible investment have different targets; text scores do not substitute for environmental or social outcomes.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / application. Frequent subcategories: Sustainability Disclosure and Greenwashing / 可持续披露与漂绿识别 (1). Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check disclosure evidence, temporal comparability, sector bias and investment constraints. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/2025.findings-acl.814.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Build traceable disclosure evidence and longitudinal evaluation, distinguishing sector exposure, report quality and actual sustainability changes.
