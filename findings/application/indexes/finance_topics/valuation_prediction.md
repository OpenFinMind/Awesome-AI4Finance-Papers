### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 预测目标包括资产价格、收益、波动率、企业价值及经营基本面。新闻、财报、市场序列和关系结构提供不同信息来源，目标与预测窗口必须明确。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Findings / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Pending review / 待核对。已标注的方法：Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (2); Causal Learning / 因果学习 (1); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (1)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 同时核对时间泄漏、预测目标、跨市场泛化和经济效用。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.1675.md) · [2](../finance_notes/2023.findings-emnlp.709.md) · [3](../finance_notes/2023.findings-emnlp.757.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 检验时间戳、公司身份和数据修订带来的泄漏，并区分预测准确率、资产排序与扣费后的经济效用。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** Targets include asset prices, returns, volatility, enterprise value and business fundamentals. News, reports, market sequences and relational structure provide different information; targets and horizons must be explicit.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / application. Frequent subcategories: Pending review / 待核对. Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (2); Causal Learning / 因果学习 (1); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check temporal leakage, target definitions, cross-market transfer and economic utility. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/2026.findings-acl.1675.md) · [2](../finance_notes/2023.findings-emnlp.709.md) · [3](../finance_notes/2023.findings-emnlp.757.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Check leakage from timestamps, company identity and data revisions; separate prediction accuracy, asset ranking and economic value after costs.
