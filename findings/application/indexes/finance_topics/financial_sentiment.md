## Research summary / 研究总结

### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 目标是金融文本中的情感、语调、立场及其对象。公司、事件和方面粒度不同；情感预测与利用情感预测股票收益属于不同任务。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Findings / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：News and Social Media Sentiment / 新闻与社交媒体情感 (1)。已标注的方法：Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (1); Generative Modeling / 生成建模 (1); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (1)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 检查标注口径、时效性、领域与语言迁移，区分情感准确率和收益效果。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../finance_notes/2025.findings-acl.962.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 研究标签口径、文化语言差异和跨期漂移，避免以市场收益倒推情感标签正确性。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** Targets are sentiment, tone, stance and their financial subjects. Company, event and aspect granularity differ; predicting sentiment and using sentiment to predict returns are distinct tasks.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / application. Frequent subcategories: News and Social Media Sentiment / 新闻与社交媒体情感 (1). Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (1); Generative Modeling / 生成建模 (1); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check annotation policy, temporal drift and domain/language transfer; separate sentiment accuracy from returns. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/2025.findings-acl.962.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Study annotation policy, cultural and language differences and temporal drift without treating market returns as proof of sentiment-label correctness.
