**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 建立可追溯披露证据与跨期评价，区分行业暴露、报告质量和真实可持续性变化。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** This task studies how sustainability information enters financing, investment and risk analysis. ESG disclosure, greenwashing, climate risk and responsible investment have different targets; text scores do not substitute for environmental or social outcomes.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Findings / core. Frequent subcategories: Sustainability Disclosure and Greenwashing / 可持续披露与漂绿识别 (3); ESG Extraction and Evaluation / ESG 信息抽取与评价 (2); Climate and Transition Risk / 气候风险与转型风险 (1). Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (3); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check disclosure evidence, temporal comparability, sector bias and investment constraints. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.1167.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.findings-emnlp.1825.md) · [3](../indexes/finance_notes/2024.findings-emnlp.387.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Build traceable disclosure evidence and longitudinal evaluation, distinguishing sector exposure, report quality and actual sustainability changes.
