## Abstract / 摘要

In financial markets, effective trading decisions require accurately interpreting unstructured information, especially news articles. However, prior work overlooks the complex news and agent interactions when making trading decisions. To address these limitations, we propose FinRAQ, which enhances the news importance scoring by modeling semantic and temporal interactions between news articles. FinRAQ incorporates a Socratic questioning stage into the debate framework, prompting agents to challenge each other’s reasoning through Socratic questions. Extensive experiments across six company stocks show that FinRAQ outperforms state-of-the-art baselines, achieving up to 38% improvement in annualized returns.
