### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 核心输出是资产集合、权重或再平衡方案。组合层面表现同时受到预测信号、约束、成本和配置算法影响，不能把全部收益归因于单个模块。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Main / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Asset Selection and Weight Allocation / 资产选择与权重配置 (41); Risk-Return and Multi-Objective Optimization / 风险收益及多目标优化 (14); Transaction Costs and Investment Constraints / 交易成本与投资约束 (4); Long-Term Wealth and Goal-Based Investing / 长期财富与目标投资 (3); Dynamic Allocation and Rebalancing / 动态配置与再平衡 (1)。已标注的方法：Optimization & Numerical Methods / 优化与数值方法 (40); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (18); Reinforcement Learning / 强化学习 (12); Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (4); Causal Learning / 因果学习 (2)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 统一成本与调仓频率，报告收益、回撤、风险及换手。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.1755.md) · [2](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-HL3TvE4Afm.md) · [3](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-PYnLd91wZY.md) · [4](../finance_notes/icml-2026-2jpMiRwsrL.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 在统一资产池、再平衡频率和成本假设下比较配置方法，考察极端行情与长期资金目标。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** The output is an asset set, portfolio weights or a rebalancing plan. Performance depends jointly on signals, constraints, costs and allocation, so aggregate gains cannot be attributed to one module without controls.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Main / application. Frequent subcategories: Asset Selection and Weight Allocation / 资产选择与权重配置 (41); Risk-Return and Multi-Objective Optimization / 风险收益及多目标优化 (14); Transaction Costs and Investment Constraints / 交易成本与投资约束 (4); Long-Term Wealth and Goal-Based Investing / 长期财富与目标投资 (3); Dynamic Allocation and Rebalancing / 动态配置与再平衡 (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Match costs and rebalancing frequency; report return, drawdown, risk and turnover. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.1755.md) · [2](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-HL3TvE4Afm.md) · [3](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-PYnLd91wZY.md) · [4](../finance_notes/icml-2026-2jpMiRwsrL.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Compare allocation methods under matched universes, rebalancing frequencies and costs, including stressed markets and long-horizon objectives.
