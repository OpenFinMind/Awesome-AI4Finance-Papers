### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 关注可持续性信息如何进入融资、投资和风险分析。ESG 披露、漂绿识别、气候风险与责任投资的目标不同，不能用文本评分替代实际环境或社会效果。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Main / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Sustainability Disclosure and Greenwashing / 可持续披露与漂绿识别 (6); Climate and Transition Risk / 气候风险与转型风险 (1)。已标注的方法：Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (5); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (3); Multimodal Learning / 多模态学习 (2); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (2); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (2)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 核对披露证据、跨期可比性、行业偏差和实际投资约束。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.352.md) · [2](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.2354.md) · [3](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-kgk0NqjsoW.md) · [4](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-OBMcxeSK5U.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 建立可追溯披露证据与跨期评价，区分行业暴露、报告质量和真实可持续性变化。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** This task studies how sustainability information enters financing, investment and risk analysis. ESG disclosure, greenwashing, climate risk and responsible investment have different targets; text scores do not substitute for environmental or social outcomes.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Main / application. Frequent subcategories: Sustainability Disclosure and Greenwashing / 可持续披露与漂绿识别 (6); Climate and Transition Risk / 气候风险与转型风险 (1). Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (5); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (3); Multimodal Learning / 多模态学习 (2); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (2); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (2). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check disclosure evidence, temporal comparability, sector bias and investment constraints. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.352.md) · [2](../finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.2354.md) · [3](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-kgk0NqjsoW.md) · [4](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-OBMcxeSK5U.md). Results from incompatible data or metrics are not pooled.

**Research opportunities (editorial).** Build traceable disclosure evidence and longitudinal evaluation, distinguishing sector exposure, report quality and actual sustainability changes.
