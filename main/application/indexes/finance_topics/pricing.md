### 中文

**研究问题与子方向。** 关注风险溢价、公允价值和定价关系。衍生品数值求解、模型校准与经济定价因子属于不同问题；预测未来成交价格与估计无套利价值需要分别评价。

**当前收录与方法。** 本页仅总结 Main / application 中的论文。当前较常见的子分类：Option and Derivative Pricing / 期权及衍生品定价 (5); No-Arbitrage Constraints and Calibration / 无套利约束与模型校准 (5); Asset Pricing and Risk Premia / 资产定价与风险溢价 (1)。已标注的方法：Optimization & Numerical Methods / 优化与数值方法 (6); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (3); Diffusion & Flow / 扩散与流模型 (1); Causal Learning / 因果学习 (1); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (1)。这些是多标签篇数，不能直接相加，也不表示性能排名。

**数据与评测。** 核验无套利约束、定价误差、对冲误差及交易假设。 代表论文的摘要、金融关联证据及原文链接见 [1](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-OenyzvFZPs.md) · [2](../finance_notes/neurips-2026-hWUODLVAFM.md) · [3](../finance_notes/icml-2025-OKbECHtO4S.md) · [4](../finance_notes/neurips-2021-CtugaUzfYw.md)；不同数据与指标不合并为统一数值结论。

**研究机会（编辑判断）。** 把数值精度、经济约束和真实交易可实现性共同纳入检验，明确模型假设的适用范围。

### English

**Problem and subdirections.** This task concerns risk premia, fair value and pricing relationships. Derivative solvers, calibration and economic pricing factors solve different problems; future-price prediction and no-arbitrage valuation need separate evaluation.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Main / application. Frequent subcategories: Option and Derivative Pricing / 期权及衍生品定价 (5); No-Arbitrage Constraints and Calibration / 无套利约束与模型校准 (5); Asset Pricing and Risk Premia / 资产定价与风险溢价 (1). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Test no-arbitrage constraints, pricing error, hedging error and trading assumptions. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../finance_notes/iclr-2026-OenyzvFZPs.md) · [2](../finance_notes/neurips-2026-hWUODLVAFM.md) · [3](../finance_notes/icml-2025-OKbECHtO4S.md) · [4](../finance_notes/neurips-2021-CtugaUzfYw.md). **Research opportunities (editorial).** Evaluate numerical accuracy, economic constraints and trading feasibility together, while making model assumptions explicit.
