**Problem and subdirections.** This task studies how sustainability information enters financing, investment and risk analysis. ESG disclosure, greenwashing, climate risk and responsible investment have different targets; text scores do not substitute for environmental or social outcomes.

**Current coverage and methods.** This page covers only Main / application. Frequent subcategories: Sustainability Disclosure and Greenwashing / 可持续披露与漂绿识别 (6); Climate and Transition Risk / 气候风险与转型风险 (1). Recorded methods: Language Models & LLM / 语言模型与LLM (5); Representation Learning / 表征学习 (3); Multimodal Learning / 多模态学习 (2); Retrieval & RAG / 检索与RAG (2); Uncertainty & Robustness / 不确定性与鲁棒性 (2). Counts overlap and are not performance rankings.

**Data and evaluation.** Check disclosure evidence, temporal comparability, sector bias and investment constraints. Representative abstracts, financial evidence and original sources are linked at [1](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.352.md) · [2](../indexes/finance_notes/2026.emnlp-main.2354.md) · [3](../indexes/finance_notes/iclr-2026-kgk0NqjsoW.md) · [4](../indexes/finance_notes/iclr-2026-OBMcxeSK5U.md). **Research opportunities (editorial).** Build traceable disclosure evidence and longitudinal evaluation, distinguishing sector exposure, report quality and actual sustainability changes.
